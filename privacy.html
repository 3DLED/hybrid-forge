Hybrid Forge

Privacy policy

Effective September 29, 2026

The short version

  • Everything you log stays on your phone. There is no account and no server.
  • Hybrid Forge sends nothing to us or to anyone else. No ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Location is used during a run you start, to measure distance and pace. Where you went is never saved.
  • Your data leaves your phone only when you send it somewhere yourself: a backup, a shared file, an email.

Who makes Hybrid Forge

Hybrid Forge is a strength and running training app made by John Moore, an independent developer. Questions about this policy go to jcmoore4410@gmail.com.

What the app keeps, and where

Hybrid Forge stores what you enter in the app's own storage on your phone. None of it is uploaded, and we never see it. That includes:

  • Your profile: the name you type, units, language, equipment and app settings.
  • Your training: workouts, sets, reps and weights, plans, notes, effort and how a session felt.
  • Your runs: distance, time, pace, and the time at each 100 metres. No map, route or coordinates.
  • About you, if you add it: bodyweight, resting heart rate, sleep hours, injuries and fitness test results.
  • Heart rate, on iPhone, if you allow it: the average, maximum and per-minute heart rate of a workout your watch recorded.

Location

When you start a run, Hybrid Forge uses your phone's location to measure distance and pace and to give spoken splits and pace alerts. It keeps doing so with the screen off until the run ends. On Android a notification says so for as long as it lasts. Location is never used at any other time, never leaves your phone, and is never saved: only the distance and times are kept.

Apple Health

On iPhone, you can let Hybrid Forge read workouts and heart rate from Apple Health, to add your heart rate to a session. It only reads, and never writes to Health. Health data stays on your phone and is never used for advertising or shared with anyone. You can turn access off at any time in the Health app or iPhone Settings.

Notifications

Reminders, the rest timer's alert and the workout card on your lock screen are created on your phone. No notification comes from a server.

When your data leaves your phone

Only when you choose, and only to where you send it:

  • Backups. Export a backup, or turn on automatic backup, and the file goes to the place you pick, such as iCloud Drive, Google Drive, OneDrive or your phone's storage. That provider holds it under its own privacy policy.
  • Sharing. Share a workout, a plan, your whole library or a summary image, and it goes to whoever and wherever you send it through your phone's share options.
  • Feedback. "Send feedback" opens your own email app. We receive whatever you choose to send.
  • Your phone's own backup. iCloud Backup on iPhone and Android's device backup may include the app's data, stored in your own Apple or Google account under their policies.

What Hybrid Forge does not do

  • It has no account and no sign-in.
  • It does not connect to the internet for anything of its own.
  • It contains no advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking tools.
  • It does not sell, rent or share your information. There is nothing to sell: we do not have it.

Deleting your data

Delete any workout or entry in the app, or erase everything at once with More → Erase all data. Uninstalling Hybrid Forge removes everything it stored on your phone. Backups and shared files you made are yours to keep or delete wherever you put them.

Children

Hybrid Forge is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. If the app ever starts sending any data off your phone, this page will say so before it does.
